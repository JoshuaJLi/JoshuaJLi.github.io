[[Where's the Bus]] was created in 24 hours as part of an online hackathon but I always wanted to expand on the idea properly so that it would become an app I could actually use.  So when I took CMPT 362 - Mobile App Development in Fall 2024 I worked with a group of 4 friends and we made our term project a redux of Where's the Bus.

The new version
- retrieves live transit information from Translink's GTFS feed
- includes a map view that shows nearby bus stops based on your current location
- sends scheduled live notifications for specific bus trips
- has a dark mode
